It's almost ClojureD! (plus exclusive discount)

Posted Fri, 24 Feb 2017 16:08:27 by Arne

Tomorrow it’s ClojureD again, the German Clojure conference. Us Berliners are especially lucky this year, as we’re not getting one, but two Clojure conferences. Coming July EuroClojure is coming to town!

If you’re also there then do come and say hi, I would love to meet you! (also, stickers!)

But maybe you live too far away to make it. In that case you can still be part of the fun by following @lambdaisland on Twitter. I’ll be tweeting about the talks all day, and in between I’ll even throw in a discount coupon!

Have a great weekend!
